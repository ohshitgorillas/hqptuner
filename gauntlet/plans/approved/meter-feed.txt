## What is wrong

No way for a page to receive metering at frame rate. The only metering surface is three band levels on the `/api/status` poll (`hqptuner/api/routes/status.py:92`, `:102`), a 1 s mean (`hqptuner/engine/bands.py:22`). No streaming response exists under `hqptuner/api/`; every router is a plain request/response one (`hqptuner/api/factory.py:78-92`).

The frames already reach the host. `MeteringReader._ingest` (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:380-389`) takes every frame (`DECIMATE = 1`, `:53`) and hands it to two consumers, the advisor aggregate and the band ring (`:386-388`). Per-channel `peak` and `rms` sit in every frame (`docs/protocol.md:292`); `frame_silent` reads `rms` alone (`hqptuner/engine/bands.py:30-36`) and nothing reads `peak`. `frame_power` sums channels (`:39-50`), so no per-channel spectrum exists.

`docs/plans/meter-mode.md:21-25` specifies the feed the METER page is to read.

## What the owner sees change

Nothing on screen: no page reads the feed yet. `GET /api/meter/feed` answers `text/event-stream` while metering is on, and 204 when it is off (`HQPTUNER_METERING_ENABLED=0`, `docs/architecture.md:98`), which an `EventSource` takes as a failed connection and never retries: WHATWG HTML §9.2.2, "if res's status is not 200 ... fail the connection", and §9.2.3, "Once the user agent has failed the connection, it does not attempt to reconnect" (https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/server-sent-events.html).

Stream shape. One `geometry` event, source Nyquist, channel count, 1/12-octave band centres, always ahead of the first `frame` event it describes; then one `frame` event per stride: per channel `peak` max-held across the stride, `rms` power-averaged across it, and the band spectrum power-averaged across it, all dB, 0.1 dB resolution. Attach while the feed holds a geometry: that `geometry` goes to the new subscriber at once. Attach before any frame, or after `reset()`, which forgets the geometry: nothing is sent until the first frame arrives, and that frame's geometry goes to every subscriber before its stride's `frame`. Channels, bin count or bandwidth changing mid-stride (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:384` rebuilds the aggregate on the same change): the partial stride of the old geometry is dropped, never sent, and the new `geometry` goes out ahead of the new stride.

Stride is frames per 40 ms of the frame time the header declares (`transformTime`, `docs/protocol.md:291`), nearest integer with ties rounding up, at least 1: 2 at 44.1k (21.5 events/s), 2 at 48k, 3 at 88.2k, 4 at 96k (23.4/s), 8 at 192k (23.4/s), 15 at 384k (25/s). A DSD source reads as its decimated base rate (`docs/protocol.md:289`), so DSD64 strides as 44.1k.

Bands: centres at 1000·2^(k/12) Hz from the first above 20 Hz (20.9 Hz) to the last whose lower edge sits below Nyquist: 122 bands at 22050, 147 at 96000. A band's level is the mean power of the bins inside its edges; a band holding no bin takes the nearest bin, so neighbouring low bands carry one bin's value — the wide bars `docs/plans/meter-mode.md:15` asks for, not interpolation. At 21.5 Hz bins that sharing runs up to about 360 Hz; above it every band holds at least one bin. Floor `BAND_FLOOR_DB` (`hqptuner/engine/bands.py:24`), -100 dB, where power is zero.

Silence: no frames reach the reader when the engine is not playing (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:341-342`) or on a DSD source with the matrix off (`docs/protocol.md:289`), so the stream sends nothing; the connection stays open. The feed resets each time `_stream` connects (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:334`), so a resume never mixes frames from before the pause, a pause shorter than one `IDLE_RECHECK` (`:49`) included.

Departure from `docs/plans/meter-mode.md:23`, "every second to fourth frame" and "About 15 KB/s": stride runs past 4 above 96k, since a fixed 2–4 would put 192k at 47 events/s; the 20–30/s rate the same line gives holds at every rate above. Per-channel spectra, which the page's summed-or-per-channel control (`docs/plans/meter-mode.md:32`) needs, put a stereo 44.1k frame event near 1.5 KB, about 33 KB/s per open page, double the doc's figure.

## Which files get touched, and roughly how

`hqptuner/engine/meterfeed.py` — new. `reduce_frame(body, channels, bins, bandwidth)`: numpy view over the frame body (numpy already runtime, `pyproject.toml:7-13`), per-channel `peak`, `rms` and 1/12-octave band power. `MeterFeed`: stride accumulator plus subscriber set. `add(header, body)` returns before reducing anything when no subscriber is attached — the doc's "sends only while a page is attached" (`docs/plans/meter-mode.md:25`), taken back to the reduction so an unattached reader pays nothing. `subscribe()` hands back a bounded queue, 8 events, oldest dropped on overflow, so a stalled client holds at most 8 events and never back-pressures the reader; `unsubscribe()`; `reset()`. Every edge reads the same for one subscriber or many: one reduction per stride, one enqueue per subscriber. New file rather than growth in `metering.py`, at 398 lines against the 500-line gate (`scripts/gates/check_file_length.py:38`).

`hqptuner/engine/metering.py` — reader owns a `MeterFeed` built in `__init__` (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:195-219`); `_ingest` (`:380-389`) calls `feed.add` beside the ring; `_stream` calls `feed.reset` beside `open_connection` (`:334`).

`hqptuner/api/routes/meter.py` — new router. `GET /api/meter/feed`: 204 when `manager.metering` is None; else subscribes, returns a Starlette `StreamingResponse` over an async generator yielding `event:`/`data:` lines, `Cache-Control: no-cache`, unsubscribes in `finally`. Disconnect is seen without a send: uvicorn declares ASGI spec 2.3 (`.venv/lib/python3.14/site-packages/uvicorn/protocols/http/h11_impl.py:207`), and below 2.4 Starlette runs `listen_for_disconnect` beside the stream and cancels it (`.venv/lib/python3.14/site-packages/starlette/responses.py:267-280`), so a page left during a pause frees its queue at once.

`hqptuner/api/factory.py` — `meter` joins the route import (`hqptuner/api/factory.py:9-23`) and one `include_router` beside `:78-92`.

`hqptuner/__main__.py` — `timeout_graceful_shutdown=5` on `uvicorn.run` (`hqptuner/__main__.py:27-31`). Uvicorn waits for open connections before lifespan shutdown, unbounded by default (`.venv/lib/python3.14/site-packages/uvicorn/server.py:287-302`, default `None` at `.venv/lib/python3.14/site-packages/uvicorn/config.py:231`); an open feed never closes on its own, so without the bound every container stop with a METER page open would run to compose's kill, `compose.yaml` setting no `stop_grace_period`, and skip lifespan shutdown (`hqptuner/api/lifespan.py:81-95`). 5 s sits under compose's 10 s default.

`docs/openapi.json` — regenerated by the gate's own `--write` (`scripts/gates/check_openapi.py:1-14`).

`docs/protocol.md` — `docs/protocol.md:294` names `hqptuner/engine/meterfeed.py` as the third runtime decoder of §7 frames.

`docs/architecture.md` — one bullet beside `docs/architecture.md:98`: the feed, its stride, and reduction only while a subscriber is attached.
