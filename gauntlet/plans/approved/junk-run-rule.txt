## What is wrong

The 20k verdict is one block's reading, sampled at poll cadence. `hqptuner/engine/junkadvisor.py:285` `_junk20k` answers off `record` alone; `hqptuner/engine/metering.py:249` hands it `block=agg.latest_block()` at the moment `verdict()` is called.

Blocks close every second, `hqptuner/engine/metering.py:59` `BLOCK_SECONDS = 1.0`; auto-pilot samples every two, `hqptuner/config.py:81` `POLL_INTERVAL`, default `2.0`. So about every other closed block reaches no rule, and one block at the cut moves the daemon: `docs/junk-filter-autopilot-resource-20k.md:275` "a track near the cut can move the junk filter on the daemon every poll".

The locked rule is a run over blocks. `:111` "engages on the second such kept block within an index distance of 3, holds through dropped blocks, and drops on the first kept block that reads real"; the ruling behind it at `:194`, "a dropped block neither confirms nor breaks the run"; gap and hold settled at `:203`, "The locked gap is 3, hold unbounded". Stage table row `:271` reads `next`.

Nothing in the tree carries the distinction the rule turns on. `_junk20k` returns `None` for a masked block (`hqptuner/engine/junkadvisor.py:292`), for a step under the cut (`:295`) and for a vetoed block (`:298`) alike, so no caller can tell a dropped block from one that reads real.

`gauntlet/plans/approved/junk-live-verdict.txt:14` "No hysteresis, no hold timer" forbids the hold, and `docs/junk-filter-autopilot-resource-20k.md:275` names the amendment owed: "line 14 forbids hysteresis and hold, and the run rule amends it".

## What the owner sees change

Auto-pilot engages 20k on the second confirming kept block, two to four seconds of coverage, instead of on the first poll that happens to catch one block over the cut.

Once engaged it stands through blocks with nothing above the fold and through silent blocks, and releases on the first kept block that reads music. A track sitting at the cut no longer moves the filter every poll.

The chip follows the same state, and clears as it does today once the engaged filter treats it. Same text, same markup.

A sample-rate change starts the run over: nothing engages until two confirming kept blocks land on the new rate.

At the other three boundaries the run stands, and each is a reading rather than a rule of its own. A pause mid-track keeps the aggregate (`docs/architecture.md:98`), so an engaged 20k stands through it and releases on the first kept block after resume that reads real. A track change at the same rate resets nothing, as the spur hold's does not (`:98`, "a held bin releases when the rules stop seeing it, not when the track changes"): an engagement bleeds into the next track until a kept block reads real, which is the bleed `docs/junk-filter-autopilot-resource-20k.md:194` accepts. The reason line names the fold of the most recent junk block in the run, not the fold that engaged it, so the ceiling on screen is never older than the last block that read junk.

`CHANGELOG.md:5` `[Unreleased]` takes one `### Fixed` entry, wording at stage 2.

Spur advice, ramp advice, the reason copy and the chip markup are untouched.
