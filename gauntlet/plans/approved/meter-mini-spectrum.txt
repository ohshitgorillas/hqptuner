## What is wrong

No level readout anywhere in the UI. `/api/status` carries metering as a bare bool — `"metering": manager.metering is not None` at `hqptuner/api/routes/status.py:101` — so the page knows metering is on and nothing about what it sees.

The frames are already on the host. `MeteringReader._ingest()` (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:356`) takes every frame and decodes per-bin power through `_frame_power()` (`:387-398`); the single consumer is the junk advisor aggregate `_agg` (`:211`), exposed by `aggregate()` (`:227-229`). Nothing else reads a frame.

The header holds one instrument, `<${ApodLamp} />` at `hqptuner/static/components/Header.js:138`.

`docs/plans/meter-mode.md:9` specifies the readout: "three band levels, low, mid and high, integrated by the backend over the poll interval".

## What the owner sees change

Three-band mini spectrum in the header, right of the apodizing lamp. Bars ease toward each poll's reading with `--sweep` (`hqptuner/static/css/base/tokens.css:241`).

Bars park at floor when the engine is not playing, when metering is off, on a 1-bit source, where the stream sends no frames, and through digital silence, which does send frames (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:378-384`, `SILENT_RMS_DB = -90.0` at `:64`) and reads as floor rather than as nothing.

No new request and no cadence change: the bars ride the existing `/api/status` poll (`hqptuner/static/store/sync.js:120`, cadence derived at `hqptuner/static/store/ui.js:26-28` — 1 s in live mode, 2 s otherwise).

Departure from `docs/plans/meter-mode.md:9`, "integrated by the backend over the poll interval": the reader integrates a fixed 1 s of frames instead, and a read neither drains nor resets it. The backend cannot see the poll interval, which is two values (`hqptuner/static/store/ui.js:26-28`); and `/api/status` has readers besides the poll — `setAutopilot` re-reads it out of band (`hqptuner/static/store/actions.js:397-399`), and each extra open page polls it — so a drain-on-read window would empty the bars under exactly the traffic that fills them.

Readout only. No click target, no navigation, no METER page, no text on the instrument.

## Which files get touched, and roughly how

`hqptuner/engine/metering.py` — band integrator fed from `_ingest()` (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:356`), which already holds the geometry it needs at `hqptuner/engine/metering.py:358`: `bandwidth, xform_time = float(header[4]), float(header[5])`, bandwidth being the source Nyquist (`docs/protocol.md:291`, "`f32 bandwidth` (Nyquist, Hz)"). New private helper sums the linear power `_frame_power()` returns (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:387-398`) over three bin spans and clamps to a floor; the reader keeps the last 1 s of those triples, 1 s counted by that `xform_time`, and `bands()` returns their mean beside `aggregate()` (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:227-229`) without mutating, `None` when the ring is empty. The ring clears wherever the reader stops streaming, which the aggregate does not: the pause branch keeps that on purpose — `keep = True  # paused: keep the aggregate for the resume` (`:291`) — and only a broken stream reaches `self._agg = None` (`:296`), so the ring is cleared on the not-playing branch as well as beside that line. A quiet stream reaches neither: `_stream` returns only on `ctx.playing` false (`:322-323`) and otherwise spins on the idle tick (`:326-327`), and a ring aged by arriving frames never ages when none arrive. `bands()` therefore ages the ring out, returning `None` once the newest triple is older than the window, which is what parks the bars on a 1-bit source entered without an intervening not-playing tick. That reading paces on a seam, not on a module-level clock, under `docs/testing.md:19-22` — "production code paces itself through injectable clock, suite virtualizes it" — which the age-out does not overturn: `MeteringReader.__init__` (`hqptuner/engine/metering.py:195-205`) takes `sleep` alone, "the test seam standing in for the reconnect backoff's wall clock" (`:204`), and gains a `monotonic` keyword beside it defaulting to `time.monotonic`, the shape `discover` already uses (`docs/testing.md:21`). The band tests inject it, so none waits on a wall clock (`:71`). `DECIMATE = 1` (`:48`), so 1 s of `xform_time` is 1 s of wall clock; a raised decimation would stretch the window by that factor. Edges: low below 250 Hz, mid 250 Hz to 4 kHz, high 4 kHz to `bandwidth` — `docs/plans/meter-mode.md:9` names "low, mid and high" and no corners.

`blockstats.py:91-101` `_per_hz_level()` is not reused: it takes a 2-D dB array and a `Grid` where `_ingest` holds one frame of linear power, and `_band_bins` returns None for any band whose top lies above `grid.hz(grid.kept - 1)` (`hqptuner/engine/blockstats.py:80-88`), with `kept = bins - DROP_TOP_BINS` and `DROP_TOP_BINS = 25` (`:28`, `:64`), which is every high band running to Nyquist. Those top bins are dropped for the advisor because they "carry the anti-imaging filter's transition, not the master's" (`:27`); the high bar keeps them, since it reports what the source sends rather than scoring it.

`hqptuner/api/routes/status.py` — the dict literal at `hqptuner/api/routes/status.py:94-103` gains `bands`, three floats or `null`. `null` when `manager.metering` is None and when `bands()` returns None. The `deps.snapshot` envelope (`hqptuner/api/deps.py:52-59`) is untouched.

`hqptuner/static/components/MiniSpectrum.js` — new. Reads `engineStatus` (`hqptuner/static/store/signals.js:23`), renders three bars, maps dB to bar height against a fixed floor, floors on `null`.

`hqptuner/static/components/Header.js` — one element beside `hqptuner/static/components/Header.js:138`, in the readout row with the lamp.

`hqptuner/static/css/base/header.css` — rule block after `.apod-legend` (`hqptuner/static/css/base/header.css:156`): bar geometry, well, and the `--sweep` transition on height. Two bindings hold and neither is overturned: tokens-only for typography, surfaces, radius, motion and spacing (`docs/design-system.md:12`), and motion's three roles (`:27`), where `--sweep` is already "live readout easing toward value poll refreshes" and names "meter fill" as its case.

`CHANGELOG.md` — one `[Unreleased]` entry, user-visible change.
