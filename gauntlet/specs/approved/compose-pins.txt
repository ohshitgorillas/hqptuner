wire and protocol facts

- Discovery is one UDP datagram to `239.192.0.199:4321`, and the answering daemon's address is the datagram's own sender address, never a field in the body (`docs/protocol.md` §2, and `hqptuner/engine/discovery.py:41-53`). A daemon that does not answer sends nothing, so an empty sweep is the deadline expiring, not an error.
- The control port refuses connections for about ten seconds after a daemon restart (`docs/protocol.md` §1), which is why `enrich` keeps a record whose `GetInfo` failed rather than dropping it.
- `GetInfo` over the control port is what names the product and platform; the discovery reply carries only the friendly name and version (`docs/protocol.md` §2, `hqptuner/engine/discovery.py:72-92`).
- Discovery is not a lane: unauthenticated, read-only, carrying no setting and no status, run before a lane exists (`docs/architecture.md:20`).
- The Docker host alias `host.docker.internal` resolves inside a container only, and only where the deployment provides it; `compose.yaml:25-26` provides it through `host-gateway`.
