brief:
> Rewrite the junk-filter detector's 20k and 50k rules to read the windowed minimum spectrum (`window_min_db()`), as the spur rule already does. Both rules currently read a mean spectrum accumulated over time; that accumulator goes, and so does the verdict latch: a verdict is a property of the current spectrum, recomputed every tick, held by nothing. The "fake lossy hi-res" case goes with it: a source that brick-walls below 20 kHz gets no verdict, because the 20k filter does nothing to it.
> No time-averaging and no memory of past verdicts survives anywhere in the detection path.
> A filter is recommended, and auto-pilot engages it, only when it has an effect on what is playing. 20k needs a content ceiling above 20 kHz with near-floor energy above it. 50k needs a container whose bandwidth reaches past the corner; derive the rate floor from that and state it.
> Auto-pilot follows the live verdict directly: engage when present, release when absent. No hysteresis, no hold timer.
> It dies with the latch.
> MY BRIEF IS EXPLICIT: EXCISE ALL TESTS THAT NO LONGER PIN A VALID CONTRACT. I AM NOT CONCERNED WITH HOW WELL-FORMED THEY ARE.
