wire and protocol facts:

- `docs/architecture.md` §3 (line 40): management credentials come from `HQPTUNER_HQP_USERNAME` / `HQPTUNER_HQP_PASSWORD` or from the saved record when those are unset or empty, and are used for HTTP Digest against 8088, realm `com.signalyst.hqplayer.embedded`. That paragraph also states "An install without them has no 8088 lane, so `ready` never turns true"; this plan corrects the sentence, because the stock defaults mean a fresh install does have a lane.
- `hqplayerd-readme.txt`: the stock management pair is `hqplayer` / `password`, and it is changed with `hqplayerd -u <username> <password>`. `hqptuner/config.py:71-72` defaults to that pair.
- `/api/health` keeps `reachable`, `ready` and `credentials_ok` apart, and `docs/architecture.md` §3 with `hqptuner/api/routes/status.py:54-57` state that the 4321 handshake carries no authentication, so `reachable` cannot speak for the 8088 lane. `ready` is the whole load with the 8088 lane answering inside it.
- `POST /api/connection` saves without verifying, deliberately, so a credential can be fixed while the daemon is down; the record is written whole, and `remember` false writes the password as the empty string.
