surface: `hqptuner/engine/junkrun.py`, carrying `read_block(record, bandwidth)`, which returns
one of three readings for one closed block — dropped, real, junk — and `JunkRun`, whose
`observe(record: blockstats.BlockRecord | None, bandwidth: float) -> None` takes one closed
block in the order the blocks close and whose `engaged_fold() -> float | None` returns a fold
in Hz or None. `hqptuner.engine.metering.SpectralAggregate` carries one `JunkRun` as
`junk_run`, and `hqptuner.engine.junkadvisor.classify` and `.verdicts` take it as the
keyword-only argument `run: junkrun.JunkRun | None` in place of `block`.

Blocks are built at 1025 bins on a grid spanning 0 Hz to 48000.0 Hz, read at bandwidth 48000.0
and samplerate 96000, from frames carrying flat bands as `tests/engine/test_junk_blockstats.py`
builds them, the record taken with `blockstats.block_record`. A band's per-Hz level is its raw
bin level less 16.7091 dB at this grid. Three inputs are named once and used by name:

- A: junk band 24300 to 30000 Hz at -118 dB per Hz, a band from 20250 to 23700 Hz 12 dB under
  the junk band's raw bin level, and a music band from 15000 to 18000 Hz at -60 dB per Hz.
- B: junk band 24300 to 30000 Hz at a raw bin level of -50 dB, the 20250 to 23700 Hz band at
  -62 dB, and the music band at a raw bin level of -40 dB.
- C: no record, `None`.

1. Engagement takes two blocks and both must read junk. Into one fresh `JunkRun`, observe B
   then A; into another, observe A then A: `engaged_fold()` read after each pair is
   (None, 24000.0).
   kills: engages on the first junk block, which is what ships today, so a track sitting at
     the cut moves the junk filter on the daemon at every poll and the listener hears the

   sequence is (24000.0, None).
   kills: holds a pending first block for as long as no real block arrives, so two junk blocks
     half a minute apart engage the filter and a fake read once in a quiet passage engages on
     evidence a track has long left behind
   bite: null stub fails at the first sequence (surface new)
   existing: none, `grep -rn "junkrun\|JunkRun\|engaged_fold\|read_block" tests/` exits 1
     with no output
3. An engaged run stands through blocks carrying no record and releases on B. Into one fresh
   `JunkRun`, observe A, A, C, C, C; into another, observe A, A, C, C, B: `engaged_fold()`
   read after each sequence is (24000.0, None).
   kills: releases on every block that does not read junk, so a silent second or a block the
     mask drops disengages the filter mid-track and the next junk block re-engages it, which
     is the flapping the run rule exists to end
   bite: null stub fails at the first sequence (surface new)
   existing: none, `grep -rn "junkrun\|JunkRun\|engaged_fold\|read_block" tests/` exits 1
     with no output
4. The corner follows the run rather than the window, and every closed block reaches the run

   frames of A; into a second, three times with the frames of A; into a third, three times

   samplerate=96000, sdm=False, run=aggregate.junk_run)`'s `filter` value once per aggregate,
