surface: `hqptuner/engine/junkrun.py`, carrying `read_block(record, bandwidth)`, which returns
one of three readings for one closed block — dropped, real, junk — and `JunkRun`, whose
`observe(record: blockstats.BlockRecord | None, bandwidth: float) -> None` takes one closed
block in the order the blocks close and whose `engaged_fold() -> float | None` returns a fold
in Hz or None. `hqptuner.engine.metering.SpectralAggregate` carries one `JunkRun` as
`junk_run`, and `hqptuner.engine.junkadvisor.classify` and `.verdicts` take it as the
keyword-only argument `run: junkrun.JunkRun | None` in place of `block`.

Blocks are built at 1025 bins on a grid spanning 0 Hz to 48000.0 Hz, read at bandwidth 48000.0
and samplerate 96000, from frames carrying flat bands as `tests/engine/test_junk_blockstats.py`

- B: junk band 24300 to 30000 Hz at a raw bin level of -50 dB, the 20250 to 23700 Hz band at
  -62 dB, and the music band at a raw bin level of -40 dB.
- C: no record, `None`.
