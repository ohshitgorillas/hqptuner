# Junk filter auto-pilot: approach and results

Reference for the junk filter auto-pilot: what each filter setting targets, the detector that currently performs best for it, how that detector scores against the labelled corpus, and which approaches lost to it. Living document until the feature is locked in. A result overwrites its predecessor only when it supersedes and improves it; a new capture round appends to the corpus section; an approach that is beaten moves to the regressions section with only enough of its data to show why it is not tried again.

Companion: `hqptuner/engine/junkadvisor.py` (classifier), `hqptuner/engine/metering.py` (spectral aggregates fed by hqplayerd's metering stream), `hqptuner/engine/blockstats.py` (per-block curves and scalars of section 2, in numpy), `scripts/junkcal_burst.py` (raw burst capture), `scripts/junkburst_blocks.py` (unpack and scoring).

## 1 · What each setting targets

| Setting | Target | Signature in the spectrum |
|---|---|---|
| 20k | Fake hi-res: 1x content permanently upsampled into an Nx container | Brick wall at the source Nyquist, 20 to 24 kHz, with images, dither or nothing above it |
| 30k, 40k | Spurs: constant low-level tones in a narrow band from recording or playback equipment | Fixed-frequency peaks above 25 kHz, tens of dB below content, hidden by transients |
| 50k | DSD-derived or heavily noise-shaped masters | Ultrasonic noise floor that rises with frequency |

A fourth class, hi-res band-limited above 24 kHz, is labelled `SHELVED` and left out of every score until ruled on; it has no member at present. Hi-res that needs no cleanup filter is labelled `CLEAN`, which grades as real in every 20k table. Engine of Hell, a wall at 44 kHz in a 96 kHz container, is one. Celestial Blues, a wall at 28.8 kHz with a spur on it in a 96 kHz container, is another: the shape of a converter with a slow roll-off at 30 kHz, Lavry-style, with a switching-supply tone in its transition band. Grief's Infernal Flower is the same converter with a cleaner top. The tone is a 30k matter (section 4), not a 20k one.

**The target is content upsampled from a 1x source.** The auto-pilot's output per track is one of `OFF`, `20K`, `30K`, `40K` and `50K`. The 20k filter engages on every master that was upsampled from 44.1 or 48 kHz, whatever sits above the source Nyquist. Where the upsampler had a filter with enough attenuation the band above the wall is empty, and the 20k filter still earns its place: the upsampling filter's own ringing sits in its transition band at the source Nyquist, and a filter whose passband ends below that band removes the ringing with it. Where the upsampler had no filter the band above the wall carries images at full level. Most masters sit between, a soft wall with attenuated images above it. All three engage. Marked for Death, a steep wall with nothing above it, is the reference for the first shape.

Real content may also stop at 22.05 kHz. A sampler or synth that runs at 44.1 kHz internally and is recorded through its analog outputs, or a mixing chain that passes through converters at 1x, leaves a wall there in a real recording. Amnesty (I) is one: a wall at 22.05 kHz, not steep, a switching-supply tone above it. Lateralus is another: certain sources in it stop at 22 kHz, and what sits above that is not a mirror of what sits below, so it is real. Both read `OFF`. What separates Marked for Death from Amnesty on the spectrum is the steepness of the wall, and nothing else: an upsampling filter with attenuation falls tens of dB inside a kilohertz, and a converter or analog chain does not.

**The cost of a wrong call is one-sided.** Engaging the 20k filter on a 1x upsample removes no music, since such a master has none above the source Nyquist; a needless engage there costs nothing. Engaging it on real content removes that content, the band from 20 kHz up. Never calling real content junk is the requirement; leaving an upsample alone is a missed benefit. Scores weight the two accordingly.

The three signatures need three different time behaviours. A spur is only visible where the music is not, so it is read from a minimum over a few seconds. A ramp is visible in a single spectrum. A wall is visible where the music reaches it, so it is read per frame and latched per track (section 2).

## 2 · Ground truth

The label is real or fake hi-res only, set by the owner per album from knowledge of the master, with a spectrum render as tiebreak. Fake means upsampled from a 1x source, and the label is the 20k target: a fake track reads `20K`, a real one `OFF`. Spurs and rising HF noise count as real. A spectrum rule is never the answer key: grading a detector against a rule on the same spectrum measures the distance between two definitions, not the detector.

**The mirror is the definition.** A 1x upsample carries nothing above its source Nyquist but images of what sits below, attenuated by the upsampler's stopband, plus whatever dither was added after. Anything above the fold that is not a mirror of the band below it proves capture at the container rate. A tone above the fold with no partner at the source rate minus its frequency is such proof: a real converter's supply whine sits there, and an upsample can only put an image there. A tone with a partner at matching level is an image and proves nothing. The relabels this rule forced are in the corpus paragraph below.

**A spur vetoes 20K.** Where the spur reading of section 4 finds a tone above the fold on a track, and no partner stands at the source rate minus its frequency for either 1x rate, the track is real and the 20k latch is blocked for it, whatever the wall finder reads. The veto's error is one-sided the right way: a spur read on an upsample leaves it `OFF`, a missed benefit, and a spur missed leaves the wall finder to decide as it would have. The 20k rule therefore depends on the spur reading, and on that reading being fast: a spur is seen in the per-bin minimum over a few seconds, not over 30 s, and the latch waits for it.

**One question per track: is there a wall at 20 to 24.5 kHz, and is it steep.** Energy above the wall is not a gate. A steep wall with an empty band above it is an upsampling filter and engages; a gentle roll-off at the same frequency is a converter or an analog chain and does not. The steepness line is read from the corpus with Amnesty (I), Paranoid Android, Mercury and Celestial Blues on the real side and Marked for Death, Guidance, Foundations of Burden and Wine Dark Sea on the fake side; it is open. The mask on energy above the fold that stood here, its level line and its ratio, is in section 6: it leaves Marked for Death, Guidance, AVOW and Amassakoul `OFF` by construction, and every one is a 1x upsample the filter must take.

**No block decides 20k.** No 1 s block, no per-block floor, share or percentile, no block verdict of any kind. The detector reads single frames and the engage is the per-track latch below. A block hides the frames that carry images behind the frames that do not. FS's block form, a floor and share of lit frames, is not the candidate; its per-frame step is. A plan, script or grade that puts a block between the frame and the latch is a defect.

**Images come and go with the music, so the engage is a latch.** On Foundations of Burden the band above 22.05 kHz fills with images only where the source is loud or transient; between transients the band is near black, and a burst taken there carries nothing to detect. The rule is therefore read per frame and the target is per track: the auto-pilot must engage `20K` on a fake album soon after the first frames with images, and it stays engaged until the track changes. A quiet burst on a fake album is not a miss and a per-burst grade that counts it as one measures the wrong thing; the grade unit is the track, with the time from the first imaged frame to the engage as its second column.

**Eyes check, blind.** Before the grade is rewritten, a stratified batch of 48 spectrograms goes to six Sonnet readers who see hashed filenames and a fixed rubric, never the label or the detector's reading: content wall frequency, band above the wall as none, faint or dense, the time spans that carry images, and a confidence. Twelve images are read twice, by neighbouring readers, to measure drift on faint against dense. The readings are joined to `labels.tsv` and to the per-frame detector output afterwards; agreement on the positives, and eyes-positive bursts the detector misses, decide whether the per-frame rule or the grade is the thing that is wrong. Batch, manifests and readings sit under `.junkburst-eyes/` at the repo root, untracked. The render's title line carries the stamp and track name, so the blind hides album and label only.

## 3 · 20k detector: floor step at a source Nyquist, and its loud-frame form

This section reads the wall at the fold on 1 s blocks; the rule reads it per frame and latches per track (section 2). Every table below carries Lateralus and Amnesty on the fake side, where both are real. The floor step lives in the scoring script only, as candidate G; the engine's block verdict is the loud-frame step G90 with the mask and the yielding veto (section 6).

**Signal.** In quiet and in loud passages alike, fake hi-res carries the source's noise floor, dither or shaped noise, up to the source Nyquist and the upsampler's stopband above it, so the floor itself steps at 22.05 or 24 kHz. A real converter floor has no reason to step there. The music above the floor is not measured at all, so the reference-band level cap and the edge-finder confusion of the previous best do not arise.

**Statistic.** Per-bin minimum over a 1 s block, 9-bin median smooth, then at each fold in 22.05 and 24 kHz the median over the 1.5 kHz band starting 300 Hz above the fold minus the median over the 1.5 kHz band ending 300 Hz below it. G is the larger absolute step of the two folds. A fold outside the burst's grid drops out. A block reads fake at 8 dB or more.

**Score, steady bursts, 1612 labelled 1 s blocks, cut chosen by minimum wrong-side in sample, then leave-one-album-out (each album graded at the cut picked over every other album):**

| Detector | Cut | Wrong | Fake called real | Real called fake | Wrong, held out |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| G, floor step | 8.1 dB | 134 | 110 | 24 | 139 |
| p90 fall, previous best | 33 dB | 230 | 205 | 25 | 343 |

**Window.** Block length does not move G's error rate. ### 3.1 Reference-band fall, previous best

**Statistic.** Per-bin 90th percentile of the summed-channel power over a 1 s block, then the existing cliff arithmetic of `junkadvisor`: content edge inside 20 to 26 kHz, fall measured from the 15 to 18 kHz reference to the median above the edge plus guard. A block reads fake at a fall of 36 dB or more. A block with no edge inside the window carries no reading.

**Window.** Accuracy is flat from 0.125 s to 5 s. Block length buys stability only: bursts with a verdict flip fall 70, 64, 50, 26, 18 across 0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2 s. The knee is 1 s.

### 3.2 Policy

**Policy.** Changing the junk filter on the running engine costs nothing: no interruption, no glitch, no silence. The 20k setting follows the per-track latch of section 2: it engages soon after the first frames that show the wall and holds until the track changes. A sample-rate change resets state at once. The 30k and 40k settings keep a hysteresis of their own on a 3 to 5 s minimum (section 4). The run rule below is the block verdict's confirming rule and its grade.

**Owner ruling on the run rule.** Engaging on real content is the one error that counts. Bleed onto a block where the filter removes nothing, a dropped block on a fake, is accepted and preferred to flapping. So the confirming block is counted over kept blocks only: a dropped block neither confirms nor breaks the run, and the filter is held through dropped blocks once engaged and drops on the first kept block that reads real. Graded in the report's `## Run rule sweep` section, gap being the largest distance allowed between the two confirming kept blocks and hold the number of consecutive dropped blocks the filter is held through, at the working rule:

| Gap | Hold | Real engaged | Fake engaged | Fake missed | No-op blocks engaged |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | any | 0 | 25 | 8 | 0 |
| 2 | any | 0 | 27 | 6 | 2 |
| 3 | any | 0 | 28 | 5 | 6 |
| 5 | any | 0 | 28 | 5 | 6 |

Gap is the index distance between the two confirming kept blocks, so adjacent blocks read gap 1. No setting engages a real burst. Gap 3 spans a 5 s burst, so gap 5 adds nothing. The hold bound never bites: no burst carries a drop run longer than 3, so hold is ungraded by this corpus; hold is unbounded, since a dropped block on a real album engages nothing and the filter drops on the first kept real block regardless. The locked gap is 3, hold unbounded. Under it Foundations of Burden at 133409Z (kept blocks 1 and 4) and 231047Z (2 and 4) and Wine Dark Sea at 81102Z (0 and 2) engage. Caress of Steel at 50041Z is not a run-rule miss: its kept blocks read 10.5, 7.3 and 15.6 dB, and the first, under the 11 dB cut, reads real and breaks the run. The five misses left are two correct `OFF`, The Patient at 16 s and Schism at 0 s, and three blocks under the cut by half a dB or less, Foundations at 133404Z and Caress of Steel at 50041Z and 45630Z. At 10 dB the cut takes them and puts 12 real blocks over the line, enough under gap 3 to engage a real burst; the cut holds at 11 dB.

## 4 · 30k and 40k, current

Per-bin minimum over about 30 s, spur peak against its baseline. The labelled corpus counts spurs as real and cannot grade the setting; it can grade the spur's presence, since a spur above the fold with no mirror partner is a real veto for 20k (section 2) and the real albums that carry one are known. Release follows the spur, not the track: a spur that fades out with its track releases the filter with it, and the filter is not held for a spur that might return. The window is 3 to 5 s, long enough for transients to fall out of the minimum and no longer; a 30 s minimum releases far too late, and the 20k veto needs the reading within seconds of the track's start. Whether a spur stays visible under a loud passage at that length is ungraded. The statistic stands; the window is what changes.

## 5 · 50k, current

Ramp slope over the top of the band on the current aggregate. The labelled corpus counts rising noise as real and cannot grade it.

## 6 · Regressions

Beaten approaches, kept so they are not repeated.

- **Energy above the fold as a gate, the mask.** A block or frame reads only where the band from 300 Hz to 6 kHz above the fold reaches -125 dB per Hz, on the premise that the filter removes nothing below that. Real content above the fold sits from -125 to -109 dB and fake blocks run continuously from -155 to -102, so the line keeps real blocks in play: 58 fake and 1017 real of 1612, 6 fake albums. The premise is wrong. A steep wall with an empty band above it is an upsampling filter whose ringing the 20k filter removes, so Marked for Death, Guidance, AVOW and Amassakoul, every one a 1x upsample, read `OFF` by construction, and a Foundations of Burden burst carries a 15 dB step on every frame with its band 26 dB under the line, unread. The target is the wall, not what sits above it.
- **Ratio to the music as a real veto, and its yield.** A kept block whose band above the fold stands within -15 dB of its 15 to 18 kHz level is forced `OFF`, yielding to a loud-frame step of 14 dB. In the policy grade the veto costs five bursts of Lateralus and gains one burst of Meddle, and the yield sits one dB over Meddle. Lateralus is real, so the yield engages real content on three bursts and the veto's gain is real blocks it should never read. Real ultrasonic content tracks the music to within about 15 dB and images sit tens of dB below it, so the ratio reads what sits above the fold, which is not the target.
- **Loud-frame step with the mask, per block, as the shipped rule.** G90 at 11 dB over kept blocks, leave-one-album-out 36 wrong, 28 fake called real, 8 real called fake; under the run rule 28 fake bursts engage, 5 miss, 0 real, Lateralus on the fake side. Per-album medians Vile Nilotic Rites 35.4 dB, The Hot Rock 26.4, Wine Dark Sea 17.1, Airbag 15.2, Foundations of Burden 12.0, Caress of Steel 10.5 against reals under 7 except Let Down 9.5: the step itself separates walls from converters, and the block and the mask around it lose the steep-wall upsamples. The per-frame step of section 2 is the same reading on single frames.
- **Per-frame fold step gated by the mask, candidate FS.** Lit gate at -125 dB per frame; a block reads junk on a floor and share of lit frames over a step cut. At floor 5, 8 dB and share 0.5: 33 fake bursts, 2 real, 93 fake missed, the two real being One Beat and Dark Side, both fake. Five albums light no frame, AVOW, Amassakoul, Amnesty, Guidance and Marked for Death, four of them upsamples the rule must take, so its gate and its block leave them `OFF`; its per-frame step is the reading of section 2.
- **Slope difference across the fold as a qualifier, candidate T as `flip`.** `above` minus `below` on the p90 curve, 1.5 kHz bands 300 Hz off the fold. Fakes whose stopband falls inside the window read negative, The Past Is Still Alive -24.2, Awaken My Love -7.2; alone it is 102 wrong blocks at 4 dB per kHz against the step's 35, and as a qualifier it gains one burst at the top of the swept range. The three-slope break of section 2 reads the wall's own fall instead.
- **A spur as evidence of an upsample.** A tone above the fold with no partner at the source rate minus its frequency cannot come from a 1x upsample and proves capture at the container rate. One Beat's four spurs above its wall and Amnesty's supply tone are real evidence, not junk to remove. Only a tone with a mirror partner is an image.
- **Block aggregation for 20k, in every form.** Per-block minimum, p90, median, floor of lit frames, share of lit frames. A block hides the frames that carry images behind the frames that do not; the shipped block rule and FS's block form both miss Foundations 133815Z, imaged in every second. The frame is the unit and the track latch is the memory.
- **The -125 dB per Hz line as a per-frame gate.** Foundations 133815Z carries a 15 dB step at the fold on every frame with its band above the fold at -151 dB per Hz, 26 dB under the line, so the gate lights none of them and the step is never read. Images the eyes call dense sit far under the level at which the filter stops mattering.
- **One frame engages the latch.** A 10 ms frame reads an 8 dB step by chance on 5 to 40 percent of real frames and 14 dB on up to 3 percent; at 14 dB a third of real tracks carry a frame over the cut, Echoes 54 of them, and one latched frame is a wrong call for the track.
- **Runs of consecutive frames over a per-frame cut.** At 10 dB a run of 47 frames is reached by 27 fake tracks and 3 real; a spur holds frame after frame as a wall does.
- **Per-frame slope break.** As noisy as the per-frame step: on frames over a 14 dB step Mercury reads p50 1.7 dB per kHz and Airbag 0.8. Gating runs on it takes the real side to 0 and the fake side from 36 to 12.
- **Two-slope break with a guard.** Slope over 2 kHz below the candidate against slope over 2 kHz above it, 250 Hz guard each side: it compares the music's slope with the plateau's and never sees the wall between them, so Hand of God, a 60 dB wall, reads about 3 dB per kHz. The three-slope form of section 2 reads the wall's own fall.
- **Confirm read from the trigger frame.** The cumulative mean at the trigger is one raw frame, so a confirm read there is the per-frame reading again and fires on 10 to 27 real tracks. A warm-up of 0.5 s before the first read takes that to 1.
- **Shelf and bump tests for a spur.** The rule carries no shelf or bump test and no special case for Mercury.
- **30 s per-bin minimum for the 20k wall.** A minimum keeps each bin's quietest frame, so quiet passages erase the wall and a loud frame cannot restore it: detection misses, release takes tens of seconds. It scores 114 wrong to the p90 block's 144 on the same 1275 blocks, so on accuracy alone it is not beaten yet; it loses on release time.
- **Lowering the cliff window bottom** to 18 or 16 kHz, reference band moved down with it. Wrong-side stays at 160 and 166 against 164 at 20 kHz, and the same albums are missed. The window is not where the misses come from.
- **Presence share above 22 or 24 kHz** (fraction of frames in the block with content above the cut). No threshold beats calling every block real: fakes carry images, dither or a noise shelf above the wall, so presence does not separate them.
- **Steepest 1 kHz fall at 20, 25 or 30 dB, and a two-row release rule**, both on the folded minimum. The fall rule flips its verdict inside an album and misses walled albums outright; the release rule never releases.
- **Self-labelled corpus** (label from a rule on the same spectrum). Grading a detector against a rule on the same spectrum measures the distance between two definitions, and every candidate reads a negative gap against it.
- **Per-burst labels on per-block readings.** A quiet block inside a real burst reads as a miss; labels must be per block.
- **Texture above the wall, three forms.** Fakes look tight above the wall on the line renders: smooth across bins, lines close together. Three statistics of that tightness over 24 to 26 kHz against 15 to 18 kHz, each as a ratio: per-bin p90 minus p10 across the block's frames (343 wrong at 1 s), p90 minus p10 of the summed band level across frames (350), and mean absolute residual of the block median after the 9-bin smooth (358). All three sit at the all-real baseline of 375 against 144 for the p90 fall, and no NaN guard helps except by dropping readings toward the majority class. The cause is the real side: most real hi-res carries nothing musical above 24 kHz either, only converter noise floor, which is as stationary and as smooth as dither. The reals whose ultrasonics carry music are the minority the eye happened to see. Real and fake differ by a step at a source Nyquist, not by texture above it.
- **Walk-up edge finder as a replacement.** Edge at the first bin 20 dB below the reference mean held for the guard width, from the reference band upward. Steady wrong 266 at a 16 dB step, 236 at 24, against 144 for the existing finder; a reference-floor guard from 8 to 16 dB changes nothing. It catches Morningrise, Orchid, The Hot Rock and Touched by the Crimson King where the existing finder walks past a hard wall, and loses Foundations of Burden, Wine Dark Sea, AVOW, White1 and Master of Puppets, where a soft wall never reaches 20 dB below the reference inside the window, and opens Is It Now? and Rage Against the Machine on notches in real music. The two finders miss different families; as a replacement it is beaten.
- **Larger of the two falls per block**, the existing finder's and the walk-up's. 229 wrong at a 55 dB cut, 207 fake called real, 22 real called fake, against 144 for the existing finder alone on the same 1275 blocks. The walk-up fall runs larger on real music than on a soft wall, so the larger-of-two reading pulls real albums over any cut low enough to catch the fakes, and the cut climbs until the fakes fall out. Combining the two finders has to be by agreement, not by maximum.
- **Floor step less the median step at control folds 27, 30 and 33 kHz** (candidate GC). On a 1275-block set, 63 wrong to G's 64, with the errors moved from the fake side to the real side, Lateralus 13 of 20 and One Beat 5 of 5. A control fold on a real converter floor is not flatter than the fold itself.
- **Floor step with its side bands subtracted** (candidate H: G's fold step minus the mean of the same two-band difference centred 2 kHz below and 2 kHz above the fold, meant to cancel a slope and keep a step). 156 wrong in sample and 176 held out against G's 134 and 139 on the same 1612 blocks. It fixes the slope cases it was built for, The Glowing Man 6 to 1, and Masterpiece 9 to 1, and breaks every soft wall whose fall spans 2 kHz or more, Foundations of Burden 2 to 9, White1 0 to 6, White2 0 to 2, and opens Is It Now? on notches, 1 to 14. A side band 2 kHz off the fold sits inside a soft wall's fall, so the subtraction cancels the wall along with the slope. The slope defect is 27 real blocks held out; no candidate has earned its cost fixing it.
- **Spread collapse above the fold** (candidate S): per-bin p90 minus per-bin minimum across frames, mean over 300 Hz to 1.8 kHz above the fold against the same band below it, on kept blocks with the veto. Fake median 0.91, real 0.95, held out 99 wrong. Real ultrasonic content in the kept blocks carries no more frame-to-frame dynamics than noise at that band width.
- **Pinned edge** (candidate P): per frame, the highest bin between 15 and 30 kHz standing 10 dB over the frame's 34 to 40 kHz mean, then the share of frames with that edge within 300 Hz of the fold. Reads 0 on nearly every block of either label: a single noisy bin near the search top clears 10 dB by chance in most frames, so the edge never lands at the fold. A stricter form, the edge being the highest bin with the whole kilohertz above it under the threshold, reads 0 on every block too, held out 58 of 58 fake blocks called real. Per-frame metering spectra do not carry a clean edge at this frame rate; the pinned edge the eye reads on a spectrogram is a many-frame average.
- **Mirror test, time-averaged** (`f_folds`): Pearson correlation between the block-median residual 0.5 to 6 kHz below a fold and its mirror above, folds at 22.05 and 24 kHz, larger of the two. No separation on any album, OK Computer included; the no-shelf family it was built for is caught by the loud-frame step or dropped by the mask (section 2).
- **Per-frame mirror on the raw spectrum** (candidate I): per frame, the Pearson correlation between the raw dB spectrum over the 6 kHz above a fold and the reversed spectrum over the 6 kHz below it, folds at 22.05 and 24 kHz, the block scalar being the share of frames whose larger correlation clears a cut. Per-album median correlation runs -0.525 (The Hot Rock, fake) to +0.162 (Schism, fake) with reals inside the same band; the share reads 0 on every album at cuts 0.5, 0.7 and 0.9, so it keeps under two blocks per burst and the grade is degenerate at every one of 27 share cuts.
- **Mirror test in time** (candidate M): per offset from 500 Hz to 6 kHz, the correlation across a block's frames between the level at fold plus offset and at fold minus offset, less the same with the upper bin shifted 1 kHz off the mirror, median over offsets. Over the kept blocks fake median 0.031, real 0.033, OK Computer 0.040, real p90 0.096; held out it calls all 98 kept fake blocks real. The band above the fold on OK Computer does not move as a mirror of the band below it. Together with the time-averaged mirror test, which also read nothing, images are not what that album carries above the fold, or the metering's frames do not resolve them.
- **Frame correlation of the above-fold band against the music band** as an images test. Pearson across a block's frames between the two band levels: fake loud median 0.64, real loud 0.75, CALIGULA 0.10, OK Computer 0.64. Both bands ride the passage's loudness swings, so everything correlates and images are not singled out. A mirror-offset form that subtracts the correlation at a bin shifted off the mirror is untried.
- **Sign of the slope above the fold** (candidate T as a sign test: fake when the p90 curve slopes up or flat over the 1.5 kHz above the fold and down over the 1.5 kHz below). Never fires: 146 fake blocks called real, 0 real called fake, the all-real baseline. Every album's median slope above the fold is negative, fakes included, because the upsampler's stopband starts falling inside the window; images do not run flat for 1.5 kHz. The difference of the two slopes is a different statistic and is open (section 3).
- **Top of the container** (candidate U, two forms: p90 level over the 2 kHz under the container Nyquist against the 15 to 18 kHz reference, and the frame correlation of that band against 100 to 500 Hz). Level: 147 wrong, no separation, fake medians -33 to -109 dB against real -4 to -91; the stopband has removed the bass image by the top of the band on every fake in the corpus. Correlation: 146 wrong; fakes read near 0, and the highest values are real, Change 0.77, Our Raw Heart 0.74, Awaken My Love 0.70 among the fakes, Celestial Blues 0.64.
- **Texture as a veto inside the fall.** A fall call vetoed to real when per-bin spread 1.5 to 4 kHz above the found edge exceeds 0.5 to 0.9 of the reference-band spread. The veto fires on fake blocks, not real ones: soft wall 122 to 150 wrong, hard wall 46 to 110, real unchanged. Dither jitter above a wall is not smaller than music spread, the same cause that beat the standalone texture candidates, so texture does not earn a place conditionally either.
- **Quiet split on level above the block floor.** The floor is the 10th percentile of the whole curve, which on a fake with a deep stopband is the stopband itself, near minus 195 dB on Guidance, so the reference sits 100 dB above it in any passage and such an album never reads quiet; the rule measures stopband depth, not music level. The absolute level rule of section 3 stands in its place.

## 7 · Engine port

The engine carries numpy as a runtime dependency (`pyproject.toml` `dependencies`), so the block arithmetic of section 2 is mirrored in numpy at `hqptuner/engine/blockstats.py` rather than ported to scalar Python. A scalar port of the same arithmetic costs what the table below reads, per `.junkburst-report-blockstats-tolerance.md`, and that cost is what the dependency answers.

| Reading | Value |
|---|---|
| Max difference, any curve or scalar | 1.7e-05 dB |
| Percentile method | linear interpolation between bracketing ranks, numpy's default; nothing else to copy |
| CPU per second of coverage, every frame, 88.2 and 96 kHz | 0.20 to 0.22 s |
| CPU per second of coverage, every frame, 192 kHz | 0.43 s |
| Peak memory per aggregate | under 13 MB |

The differences sit at float32 rounding noise, so the port tolerance is 1e-4 dB per bin and per scalar, an order over the largest reading. CPU scales with frames per second, not bins: bursts carry 1025 bins at every rate and the 192 kHz bursts carry twice the frames of the 96 kHz ones for twice the cost. That cost is for the block statistics alone at every frame, which is the rate the engine ingests at and the rate the locked cuts were read at.

Shipped as slug `junk-block-stats` at `8ad47fda`: `hqptuner/engine/blockstats.py` in numpy, `SpectralAggregate` closing 1 s blocks into a record of minimum curve, p90 curve and the three scalars, 30 blocks of reach so the spur and ramp rules read what they read before, `latest_block()` with no consumer yet. numpy in the dev container, Python 3.12, numpy 2.5.3:

| Reading | Value |
|---|---|
| Import time, one cold sample | 40 ms |
| Installed size | 70 MB |
| CPU per second of coverage with numpy, 88.2 and 96 kHz | 0.0031 to 0.0032 s |
| CPU per second of coverage with numpy, 192 kHz | 0.0063 s |

The shipped mirror agrees with the scoring package within 1e-5 dB on the minimum curve, `above_db` and `ratio_db` at every rate the corpus holds. The record's `p90` is the raw per-bin percentile, and the rule reads the step on `blockstats.p90_smoothed`, its 9-bin median smooth, which is the curve `jbedge.p90_curve` returns; raw and smoothed differ by 8 to 26 dB per bin, widest at 192 kHz. The frame correlation scalar the scoring package carries has no field in the record and none is owed.

No test pins the block median across frames for the scalars, and no test compares the mirror against the scoring package; that comparison is an oracle fixture under `tests/support/fixtures/`, its own slug, not yet built.
