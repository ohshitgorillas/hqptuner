# Phase 5: Meter playhead on the loop's dt - Plan 2: Smooth spectrum through a replayed capture

## Goal

The meters, the spectrum and the spectrogram move smoothly in step with the music while frames reach the metering reader in bursts, with the stamps still the only authority for where the music is (owner approval, 2026-11-07). This plan closes UAT item 5 (AC5). It moves the playhead's cap to the newest queued frame's end as the owner's rewritten D-01 orders, and it proves smooth spectrum playback with an automated check, not by a person's eye.

## Must be true when done

- Between arrivals the playhead keeps moving on `dt` through the queued, unshown music and stands still only at the newest queued frame's end. Through a gap it moves at wall speed: a playhead past a target the gap froze is not ahead of the music and is not slowed for it. Behind the target after an arrival it closes the lag faster than wall time. It slows below wall speed only where D-01 says: ahead of the target after an arrival, or after a delay raised mid-feed (D-03). Where the queued music runs out before the next arrival, it moves at wall speed up to the newest queued frame's end and stands there until the next arrival, with no early slowdown. It never moves backward outside a break and never passes the newest queued frame's end. Model-lane tests for each new or changed claim fail by assertion against the `playhead.js` this plan starts from and pass after the change.
- `docs/spec/timing.md` and the `hqptuner/static/model/gauges/playhead.js` header state that rule as rule text.
- A real capture of the meter feed, taken read-only from the dev container while the owner's music plays, is in the repo. Its arrivals come in bursts (at least five arrival gaps over 100 ms), and the effective delay the owner watches is recorded with it.
- The owner's limits for a smooth spectrum are recorded in the owner's own words, in units the spectrogram's scroll can read, and the browser test reads them from one place.
- Replaying that capture into a real browser at the recorded delay, on the page's own clock made virtual, the painted spectrogram's scroll stays inside every owner limit on the new playhead, and fails by assertion against the pre-phase-5 playhead (the `playhead.js` and `loop.js` of commit 6438e843).
- The browser test runs unattended in the `e2e` job: no live daemon, no fixed sleep, and its wall time does not grow with the capture's length beyond the paint work.
- `scripts/gate.sh make check` prints `exit=0`, and the existing meter e2e files still pass.
