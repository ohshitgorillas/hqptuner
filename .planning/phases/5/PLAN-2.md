# Phase 5: Meter playhead on the loop's dt - Plan 2: Bursty capture and the playhead's timing rule

## Goal

The meters, the spectrum and the spectrogram move smoothly in step with the music while frames reach the metering reader in bursts, with the stamps still the only authority for where the music is (owner approval, 2026-11-07). This plan holds the inputs the proof needs and the rule the code follows: a real bursty capture of the meter feed, the owner's ruling on what smooth means, and `docs/spec/timing.md` stating the playhead's rule. Plan 3 (`PLAN-3.md`) builds the playhead to that rule and proves, in general and on every view, that nothing stutters. Together they close UAT item 5 (AC5).

- The owner's ruling is recorded verbatim: smooth means no stutter at all, not a tolerated amount; the delay is the buffer; the proof covers stutter in general, not one symptom. (Done; `reports/plan-2.md` and Task 2 below.)
- `docs/spec/timing.md` states, as rule text, the playhead's rule from the revised D-01 and nothing beyond it: it moves at wall speed and jumps by a changed delay. Rule 2's list of the program's own timing constants names no playhead constant, because the jump leaves `CATCH_UP_MS` no role and Plan 3 deletes it.

- CONTEXT D-01, revised by the owner and locked. The rule, owner-approved: "The playhead moves at wall speed. When the delay changes, it jumps by the change: back for a raised delay, forward for a lowered one. It never passes the newest queued frame's end, and if it reaches it, it stands there until the next arrival. At a break it restarts one delay behind the newest frame's end." The delay's worth of queued, unshown music is the buffer that carries the playhead through a burst gap, and no other buffer, margin, window or tuning constant is added. Arrivals do not steer the playhead: a burst landing early or late changes nothing about its speed. A delay change is the one exception to smooth, in-time motion (owner ruling, 2026-11-07): the playhead jumps by the change at once and then moves at wall speed, and no constant sets how it gets there. The CONTEXT AC1 return to the target after a stand is dropped: once the playhead has stood at the newest end, it moves on at wall speed from the next arrival. AC4 now reads "the playhead never passes the newest queued frame's end, and never moves backward except at a break or a raised delay".
