# Phase 5: Meter playhead on the loop's dt - Plan 3: Nothing stutters while the buffer holds

## Goal

The meters, the spectrum and the spectrogram move smoothly in step with the music while frames reach the metering reader in bursts, with the stamps still the only authority for where the music is (owner approval, 2026-11-07). This plan builds the playhead to the rule Plan 2 Task 3 puts in `docs/spec/timing.md`, and proves, in general and on every view that reads the playhead, that nothing stutters while the arrivals stay inside the buffer. It closes UAT item 5 (AC5) with Plan 2.

## Must be true when done

- The playhead moves at wall speed: on every paint it moves the paint's wall time, unless it reaches the newest queued frame's end, where it stands until the next arrival and then moves on at wall speed, with no catch-up for the time it stood. No arrival changes its speed: a burst landing early or late moves it no differently. So on every feed whose gaps the delay's queued music carries, the committed real capture among them, it never stands still, never jumps and never slows. It never passes the newest queued frame's end and never moves backward outside a break (AC4). This holds over generated bursty feeds (gap lengths, burst sizes, arrivals early and late against one another, delays, steady and uneven paint rates) and over the capture. Model-lane tests for each new or changed claim fail by assertion against the `playhead.js` this plan starts from and pass after the change.
- The playhead changes speed only while it reaches a changed delay: slower until a raised delay is reached (D-03), faster until a lowered one is reached, without waiting for a break (AC2), never jumping and never backward. At a break it restarts one delay behind the newest frame's end (D-04). Two paint rates converge once both stand at the newest end (D-02, AC3). `CATCH_UP_MS` stays the only timing constant `playhead.js` defines.
- Through the meter loop, the playhead signal and the scene handed to the meters' and the spectrum's painters move on every paint in the same way. The spectrogram's edge and the apodizing strip's edge are that playhead.
- In a real browser, replaying the real capture at the delay the owner watches and generated bursty feeds at their own delays, the painted spectrogram never stands still while music plays and never jumps. On the capture no paint scrolls it further than its wall time covers, rounded up to a whole column. The browser test fails by assertion against the pre-phase-5 playhead (the `playhead.js` and `loop.js` of commit 6438e843), and its stand-still cases also fail against the playhead this plan starts from.
- The `hqptuner/static/model/gauges/playhead.js` header and doc comments state the rule as `docs/spec/timing.md` does.
- The browser test runs unattended in the `e2e` job: no live daemon, no fixed sleep, and its wall time does not grow with a feed's length beyond the paint work. `scripts/gate.sh make check` prints `exit=0`, the existing meter e2e files still pass, and the owner has the hand-back URL.

  The method (planner's choice under D-01 and the CONTEXT flagged assumption, which leaves the shape of reaching a changed delay to the planner). So a stand never turns into a catch-up afterwards (planner's choice, recorded in Notes). A restart clears it, because a restart stands the playhead at the delay it runs at.
