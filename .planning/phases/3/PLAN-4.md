# Phase 3: Windows upgrade install - Plan 4: The Arm64 tray judgment runs on a desktop that has a taskbar

## Goal

Installing a new version over an existing one leaves an app that starts cleanly: after the upgrade over v1.20.1 it shows its tray icon and no "Unhandled exception in script" dialog, and `smoke-windows` proves that on both x64 and Arm64 on a desktop where a tray icon can exist, so a red tray half means the app failed and not the runner.

## Must be true when done

- The cause of the arm64 tray failure is recorded in this plan's executor report: `WWAHost.exe`, the runner's first-logon privacy screen, is alive on `windows-11-arm` and Explorer refuses every `NIM_ADD` while it runs (Task 1 record, owner-approved amendment in Notes).
- On both installer rows of `smoke-windows` (x64 on `windows-latest`, arm64 on `windows-11-arm`), the healthy upgraded app in steps 8 and 9 passes `judge_upgraded` exactly as f476c720 left it: alive across 5 s, no visible top-level window, and a `SystemTrayIcon`-class window that `Shell_NotifyIconGetRect` answers S_OK for (ROADMAP criterion 1, UAT item 7).
- The broken-tray control runs in step 8 on both installer rows, on arm64 for the first time, and passes only on a rejection that names "Unhandled exception in script" (UAT item 7, dialog half).
- The smoke judges the tray only on a runner desktop that has a taskbar and no first-logon window open. A runner desktop that does not reach that state fails the row with its own message naming the runner's desktop; the tray half is never skipped, passed or weakened on any row.
- On the phase's last commit neither `release.yml` nor `scripts/probes/` carries the tray probe: both are as they were at faeca70d.
- `check`, `e2e`, `smoke`, `lifecycle-deb`, `lifecycle-rpm`, `smoke-macos` and `smoke-windows` all report success on the phase's last `dev` commit; the arm64 installer row finishes inside the unchanged 15-minute `timeout-minutes`; steps 1 to 7 and the `windows-bundle-x64` row are unchanged.
