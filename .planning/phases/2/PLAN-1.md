# Phase 2: Modulators fit the output rate - Plan 1: Modulator lists gray what the rate in force cannot run

## Goal

The modulator picker offers only modulators the running output rate supports: a modulator whose rate floor is above the output rate in force is listed grayed with a reason and cannot be picked, and every other modulator is picked and applied as before.

## Must be true when done

- With the SDM output rate in force at DSD256 (11289600, by pin or by the running SDM rate limit), opening the modulator list from the Shaping chain picker shows every modulator whose floor is above that rate (for example a 512+fs one at 22579200, AHM7EC8B at 40960000) grayed, with the owner-approved reason visible on the row without hovering.
- Tapping a grayed modulator does nothing: no pick, no live write, no staged edit, and the list stays open.
- In the same state, a modulator whose floor is at or below the rate in force (for example a 256+fs one at 10240000), or one with no floor, is not grayed, and picking it from the chain picker applies it live as it does today.
- The Setting Switcher slot list, the drawer nameplate list and the Snapshot builder list show exactly the same grayed rows as the chain picker.
- A Setting Switcher slot on the Modulator target that already remembers an above-floor modulator shows grayed, and tapping it sends no write; a slot remembering a supported modulator taps exactly as today.
- With PCM output running, no modulator is grayed. Stopped or on auto, the SDM rate in force is still the SDM pin or the running SDM rate limit, and the list is judged by it. With no SDM pin and no running SDM rate limit, or a 0, no modulator is grayed. The device's announced rates never gray a modulator.
- `scripts/gate.sh make check` prints `exit=0`, and the owner has seen the grayed rows at the hand-back URL.
