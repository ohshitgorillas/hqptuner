# Phase 7: Visuals stay in sync after a track starts - Plan 2: The one playhead follows the stamps' line

## Goal

After a track starts, the visuals keep the same lag behind the sound that they hold for the rest of the track, whatever the track's sample rate. This plan is the fix: the one paint decision runs the approved rule (`docs/spec/timing.md`, "The two clocks", Rules 1 and 5; `.planning/phases/7/DESIGN.md` sections 2, 4 and 5), and Plan 1's tables pass unedited.

## Must be true when done

- With music playing at the hand-back URL, the owner plays tracks at two sample rates, with a start from stopped, a same-rate track change and a rate change. The owner sees the meters, the spectrum and the spectrogram hold the same lag behind the sound right after each start as later in the track (AC6).
- Plan 1's tables pass, and none of their files changed since Plan 1's last commit (AC3, AC4, AC5 and AC9, as D-14 and D-16 state them).
- The playhead keeps every promise of the approved text. It moves at wall speed and follows the line by a bounded share of it. It takes each rise of the line at once only through the opening span after a break. It jumps by a changed delay. It never passes the newest queued frame's end, and never moves backward outside a break or a raised delay. At a break it restarts one delay behind the newest frame's end, carried on at wall speed from the time that frame arrived.
- The line the paints have seen carries through the loop from one paint to the next: a paint that takes no arrival follows the line the earlier paints saw.
- Every existing test rewritten here states a sentence of the approved text in place of the old rule's. Each asserts it exactly, on whole-unit input whose answer the test writes (rules 13 and 18), and was red against the pre-change code by assertion. A test whose input only placed the playhead for its own assertion keeps that assertion, with input under which its premise holds exactly. Contract 1's wall-speed check and the e2e wall-time scroll cap are gone, retired by the owner.
- `scripts/gate.sh make check` prints `exit=0`, and the e2e suite passes (AC7).
