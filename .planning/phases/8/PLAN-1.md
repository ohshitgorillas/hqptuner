# Phase 8: Visuals stay in sync after a track starts - Plan 1: The one playhead follows the stamps' line

## Goal

After a track starts, the visuals keep the same lag behind the sound that they hold for the rest of the track, whatever the track's sample rate. This plan is the fix: the one paint decision runs the approved rule (`docs/spec/timing.md`, "The two clocks" and Rules 1 and 5), and the 12 red tables written for it pass unedited.

## Must be true when done

- The 41 cases of `playhead-lag.test.js`, `playhead-motion.test.js`, `playhead-scroll.test.js` and `playhead-line.test.js` pass, and none of those four files changed since `63af5055` (AC3, AC4, AC5, AC9 as D-14 and D-16 state them).
- After a start, the playhead stands one delay behind the line its arrivals set, carried on at wall speed from the time the newest frame arrived, and takes each rise of the line at once through the opening span. Later it moves at wall speed and follows the line by a bounded share. It jumps by a changed delay, never passes the newest queued frame's end, and never moves backward outside a break or a raised delay.
- The line carries through the loop from one paint to the next: a paint that takes no arrival follows the line the earlier paints saw.
- No test of the old rule ("moves exactly its wall time", "arrivals do not steer it", "restarts at the newest end less the delay") is left in the tree. Every promise the approved text keeps is still held by a named test.
- `scripts/gate.sh make check` prints `exit=0` and `scripts/gate.sh make test-e2e` prints `exit=0` (AC7). The dev build is rebuilt and healthy on `:8090`.
