Wire facts:

- Zero-order hold: magnitude |sinc(f / fs_out)|, nulls at every multiple of fs_out, 3.9224 dB down at fs_out / 2, gain 2/pi. docs/plans/filter-primer-math.md:184, sourced to the zero-order hold article cited there.
- Analog low-pass: n-th order Butterworth, gain squared 1 / (1 + (f / fc)^(2n)), 6n dB per octave. docs/plans/filter-primer-math.md:188. Order and corner of a NOS DAC's output stage are unsourced and listed among the figures not to use at docs/plans/filter-primer-math.md:199; the primer's stated assumption is n = 2, fc = 50 kHz, and the card names the stage as illustrative.
- Cascaded linear stages multiply, so the stage in dB is the sum of the two, exact. Same reference. The low-pass term depends on frequency alone, so it cancels from line 1's differential and the 0.70 and 1.62 dB figures there are the hold's alone. It does not cancel from line 6, which reads one frequency across four output rates, but it is the same constant -0.110 dB at 20 kHz in all four and so does not affect the ordering.
- The frequency plot is 734 units wide from x 30 to x 764 and 181 units tall from y 39 to y 220, over a dB span of +6 to -120, so it is 1.4365 plot units per dB. hqptuner/static/components/primer/frame.js:12,15,17,24,25; the same rectangle is encoded at tests/js/support/primermatrix.js:105.
- The source rates offered are 44100, 96000 and 192000 Hz and the output ladder floors at 48000 Hz and ceils at 384000 Hz. hqptuner/static/store/primergraph.js:22,26,27.
- HQPlayer's own documentation says nothing about DAC-side analog reconstruction; no filter specification is read out of the engine for this.
