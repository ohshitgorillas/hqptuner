wire and protocol facts:

- GET /api/health serves reachable, ready, connected, unreachable_since, connected_at, info, release, license, app_version and credentials_ok (hqptuner/api/routes/status.py:29-58).
- credentials_ok is tri-state: null until the 8088 lane has answered, and only ever written when it has (hqptuner/core/readings.py:57, hqptuner/lanes/http/forms.py:90-92). Its false is a refusal, its null is silence.
- GET /api/connection answers host, username, remember and has_password, never the password (hqptuner/api/routes/connection.py:52-58).
- POST /api/connection takes host, username, password and remember, all optional, each defaulting to what HQPTuner is using now; it saves before verifying anything, and answers 400 store_unwritable when the record cannot be written (hqptuner/api/routes/connection.py:27-38, :90-98).
- GET /api/discover answers a list of records with address, name, version, product and platform; product and platform default unset and stay unset when the per-daemon control exchange fails (hqptuner/api/routes/discovery.py:19-27, hqptuner/engine/discovery.py:33-38, :70-88).
- HQPTuner's connection manager is constructed unreachable and connects asynchronously (hqptuner/core/manager.py:86,102), so the first health reading of a healthy install says reachable false.
- The health poll's cadence is not fixed: it rides refreshFast, rescheduled off fastPollMs, 1 s on the volume page, in LIVE and on System with quick updates, 2 s otherwise (hqptuner/static/store/sync.js:73-74, :122-131).
