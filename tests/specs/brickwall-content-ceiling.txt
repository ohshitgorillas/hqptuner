The content ceiling a track is described by is the frequency where its level last stands 8 dB above its own noise floor. For every input below that frequency is 15000 plus 82000 divided by the fall rate in dB per kHz, or the cutoff itself where the level drops to the floor in one step. Each expected ceiling below is that arithmetic, not a reading taken from any implementation. Tolerance 0.2 kHz, which is four bins at this bandwidth.
