Excision block grammar (the input lines 1-3 construct):

A `kind: excision` block carries excision lines in place of behavior lines, one per target:

    N. excise <target>
       rule: docs/testing.md rule <n>
       assertion: <the offending assertion, quoted from the test file>

`<target>` is `tests/<file>::<test>` for a single pytest test, `tests/js/<file>::"<test title>"` for a node one, or `tests/<file>` with no `::` for a whole file. "A whole-file target" in lines 1-3 below is that third form.
